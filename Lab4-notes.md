# Lab 4 — CAPE

Open the app. The skew-T stays on the left. The right panel is the current step.

**Before you measure, predict.** The orange and blue fills stay visible; the numbers stay hidden until you Reveal. Going back does not wipe what you wrote.

## 1 Orient

## 2 Orange

If you lift a surface parcel, where is it warmer than the air around it? Where is it cooler?

1. Follow the dashed parcel to the LCL, then up.
2. Mark the orange fill. Mark the blue fill (if any).
3. Note LCL, LFC, EL.

## 3 Predict

Looking only at the orange area: weak, moderate, strong, or extreme?

1. Predict first.
2. Reveal.
3. Compare your call to the number.

Write here: your call vs. the number. If you missed, what about the area did you misread?

## 4 Heat

If the ground heats 3 °C and dewpoint stays put, does the orange area grow, shrink, or stay put?

1. Predict: grow / shrink / little change.
2. Raise T by 3 °C.
3. Reveal.

Write here: what the orange area did, and why.

The app also shows √(2 CAPE). Is that an observed updraft?

## 5 Dry

Reset, then drop dewpoint by 5 °C. What happens to the orange area? the blue area? LCL?

1. Reset parcel.
2. Predict: grow / shrink / little change.
3. Lower Td by 5 °C.
4. Reveal.

Write here: what drying did to the orange and blue areas.

## 6 Cap

Orange aloft, blue below — why both? What does +6 °C of surface heating do to the blue area?

1. Find the warm jog in T near 850–800 hPa.
2. Predict, then Reveal.
3. Predict what +6 °C does to the blue fill.
4. Raise T by 6 °C. Reveal.

Write here: what the blue area is, and what heating did to it.
